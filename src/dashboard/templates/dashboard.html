{% endif %} {# ── Novelty callouts ── #}
N1
Novelty 1 — Action Parameter Variation
ParameterizedDQN outputs 45 Q-values (15 actions × 3 intensities). At intensity 0 (low), ssh_brute_force sends 2 attempts — below Suricata's 5-attempt threshold. The network learns to prefer this intensity because it yields lower anomaly scores and thus higher shaped reward.
N2
Novelty 2 — Auto-Remediation
After each run, auto_remediate() identifies double blind spots (evaded Suricata AND anomaly_score < 0.3). Their feature vectors are cached; future similar actions get a cosine-similarity score boost — closing gaps without retraining the IsolationForest.
